Your poker sessions, stored in your own Airtable base. Nothing leaves your device except the calls to Airtable.

How do I get these?
  1. Go to airtable.com/create/tokens and create a personal access token.
  2. Give it the scopes data.records:read, data.records:write and schema.bases:read.
  3. Under “Access”, add only your PoTracker base.
  4. The base ID is the app… part of the base URL.

The token is kept in this browser’s local storage and is never sent anywhere but api.airtable.com.
